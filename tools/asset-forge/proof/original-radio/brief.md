# Portable lakeside field radio

An ORIGINAL static prop: a small portable field radio for a lakeside hangout. No existing Quaternius item is the target shape. Warm timber enclosure, chunky muted-iron corner protection, one iron carry handle, a recessed slatted speaker grille on the left of the front face and three simple metal control knobs on the right. Keep a solid dark recess behind the grille and visible depth at panel/frame contacts. No letters, logos, glowing display, extra tools, chains or decoration. Simple substantial game-scale parts; the style must come only from the fixed family/material/contact reference boards. Approximate total dimensions including handle:55cm wide,40cm high,24cm deep including projecting controls. The original concept used an approximate 22cm depth; the modeled total is deliberately 24cm.
