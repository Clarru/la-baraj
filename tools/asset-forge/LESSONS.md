# Asset Forge failure cases

These are observed defects with executable regressions, not a claim of autonomous model training. Reuse a successful construction method; diagnose a new mismatch before changing prompts or loosening budgets.

| Observed failure | Cause and correction | Retained check |
| --- | --- | --- |

| Every agent writes the same lengthy audit from scratch | Build validity, past failures, review notes and evidence already exist as structured files. | `report` generates JSON/Markdown from those records, reports stale inputs honestly, and refuses overwriting an existing output. It does not invent visual conclusions or reconstruct shell history. |

`requiredJoints` is an authoring contract. Every multi-part custom assembly needs declared critical connections; a new recipe must supply the corresponding probes. This verifies the specified surfaces after optimization and catches the demonstrated gaps. It does not discover every possible assembly mistake, guarantee physical manufacturing joints, or replace a visual review.

Fixed style references and exact prompt records make experiments comparable. The next useful improvement must demonstrate a named defect, a correction and evidence that the correction works. The CLI reuses an unchanged checked build; it does not currently cache individual failed stages or automatically choose a repair.

The coplanar advisory projects co-facing axis-aligned triangle pairs, excludes shared edges and opposing contact faces, and samples visibility from the surface normal. It records positions, normals and overlap areas and produces marked front/rear renders. It has a 20-micrometre plane tolerance, 2-square-millimetre area floor and bounded pair/finding counts. These are declared diagnostic limits for the tested prop scale, not universal mesh-quality thresholds. Angled faces, unsampled visible regions, UV seams and shading/material errors can escape it. Findings on an underside or intentionally layered construction still need interpretation; the original accepted tackle box remains a control with residual findings.
