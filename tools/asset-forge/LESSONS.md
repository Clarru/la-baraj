# Asset Forge failure cases

These are observed defects with executable regressions, not a claim of autonomous model training. Reuse a successful construction method; diagnose a new mismatch before changing prompts or loosening budgets.

| Observed failure | Cause and correction | Retained check |
| --- | --- | --- |
| White slits between crate boards | Separate surfaces left openings. Use six continuously recessed sections with an opaque backer. | Old export fails 24 of 72 surface probes; corrected export passes all 72. |
| Metal corner protectors look like unattached plates | Three independently beveled boxes leave breaks at their meeting edges. Build one closed trihedral wrap, then bevel its complete boundary. | Eight corner contracts, three seam directions each. Previous plates fail all 24 probes. |
| Housing lid and walls look detached | Beveling each touching board cuts away both sides of the join. Build a continuous hollow shell and bevel its actual external corners. | Four housing contracts, three samples each. Previous shell fails all 12 probes. |
| A connected housing gains rectangular texture patches | Per-face UV phases restart across coplanar grid subdivisions. Share physical projection origins/phases where the trim permits; split only where the atlas width requires it. | Inspected actual textured and unlit exports; geometry probes do not assess UV continuity. |
| Radio meets triangle limits but looks poor | Primitive placement, shallow fittings, absent divider, stretched grain and a deep speaker void were artistic/construction failures. | Separate technical and visual review; retain concept and real renders, including clay and alternate lighting. |
| A visually rejected result can still be packaged | A successful lint was being treated as sufficient. | A recorded rejection blocks packaging until a revised current build is inspected and accepted. |

`requiredJoints` is an authoring contract. Every multi-part custom assembly needs declared critical connections; a new recipe must supply the corresponding probes. This verifies the specified surfaces after optimization and catches the demonstrated gaps. It does not discover every possible assembly mistake, guarantee physical manufacturing joints, or replace a visual review.

Fixed style references and exact prompt records make experiments comparable. The next useful improvement must demonstrate a named defect, a correction and evidence that the correction works. The CLI reuses an unchanged checked build; it does not currently cache individual failed stages or automatically choose a repair.
