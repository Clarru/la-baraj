# Live-room verification — bending wake and remote boats (30 October 2026)

Two isolated browser contexts (Playwright, 1280 × 720) ran the real game bundle
against a room Worker started locally with `wrangler dev --local` on port 8790
(`ALLOWED_ORIGINS=http://127.0.0.1:4173`, RAM-only Durable Object). The page was
`tests/browser/boat-live-fixture.tsx`, built by
`node scripts/prepare-boat-live-fixture.mjs http://127.0.0.1:8790` and served
from `dist/boat-qa/` by the single `npm run preview` on 4173. The fixture only
parks the local boat at a chosen pose and teleports the player beside it;
boarding (`V`), throttle (`W`), steering (`A`) and all presence, impact and wake
code are the normal game paths. No production Worker or saved world state was
touched; the preview, the fixture folder and the Worker were removed afterwards.

Both clients reported `status: online`, `localPreview: false`, each saw the
other as a remote with `vehicle: "boat"` and the staged hull pose, and both
console error logs were empty in every run.

## Turning wake (`scenario=turn`)

Player 1 staged at (−12, −60) facing +z; player 2 at (6, −52) facing −x so its
camera looked across player 1's path.

- Player 1 held `W` for 3.5 s (0 → 7.7 m/s), then `W`+`A` for 3 s: the hull
  curved from heading +z to heading +x at 8.0 m/s, passing from (−12, −47) to
  (3.4, −37.6). Captures: `captures/live-wake-turning-a.png` (own view during the
  turn), `live-wake-coasting-a.png` (1.2 s after releasing the keys, 7.6 m/s —
  the V ribbon bends back along the arc the boat actually took),
  `live-wake-faded-a.png` (4.7 s after release — the far end has faded out, the
  near end still follows the transom).
- Remote view: `captures/live-wake-remote.png` (player 2's camera) shows player
  1's LOD hull, the seated driver with hands on the wheel, the name badge and the
  wake trailing behind it along its path; `live-wake-turning-b.png` shows the
  same remote boat passing close by. Player 2's own wake in
  `live-wake-coasting-b.png` and `live-wake-faded-b.png` curves with its heading
  change as well.
- Remote pose positions received on player 2 matched player 1's own position to
  within one pose interval throughout (`remoteAonB` column of the run log).

## Incidental bump observation (not in scope)

A rear-push run (`scenario=rear`, player 1 behind player 2, both aboard and
player 2 idle) recorded the shared impact: player 1 reached 5.4 m/s, contact at
t ≈ 2.5 s dropped it to 2.9 m/s and gave player 2 2.8 m/s forward
(`captures/live-rear-bump-a.png`, `-b.png`). After a second contact at
t ≈ 3.5 s player 1 stayed at 0.0–0.04 m/s and exactly z −50.76 while `W` was
held and re-pressed, while player 2 coasted away from 3.4 to 0.7 m/s over 8 s.
In one turning run player 2's boat, with no key pressed, started moving when
player 1 passed close by and accelerated smoothly to 7.8 m/s; a second identical
run did not reproduce it. Both are pre-existing authoritative-impact behaviour
(`apps/server/boat-collisions.ts`, `boat-collision.ts`), untouched by this
branch, and are recorded here for the collision owner rather than investigated.
