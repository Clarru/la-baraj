# La Baraj — world solids and assigned homes

Cabin exterior/cutaway and furniture collisions use cached, merged fixed meshes derived from the actual displayed prefab. Model props are solid at their rendered placement and scale. Docks and parked scooters have inexpensive physical shapes; the scooter collider is disabled while mounted. Rocks and bushes use rendered model geometry, with trunk colliders for trees. Nature colliders activate within 85 metres and retire beyond 100 metres, independently from visual LOD. Grass, flowers and fire effects remain non-solid.

The character uses stronger surface grip and deceleration, a wider ground probe and a higher jump. Grounded narrow-edge contacts take precedence over a center ray that sees lower terrain. A desktop check completed successive jumps from porch to sloped A-frame roof to ridge, with stable idle footing. Actual-GLB physics tests verify all six roofs and a capsule landing on a table. This does not make vertical walls walkable or provide server-authoritative movement.

Homes use the private handshake described in LIVE_MULTIPLAYER.md. The server assigns random unused plots to distinct anonymous owners and persists credential digests. Arrivals, reset, minimap and home marker follow that assignment. All 30 arrival/scooter positions are tested dry, beyond steps and outside rendered road edges. Repeated snapshots and reconnects do not teleport an already moving player. Same-browser tabs share the same anonymous owner; different browser credentials get different plots. Clearing browser storage loses that resume capability and leaves its plot reserved; no login/recovery system is provided.

Validation for the integrated candidate: 107 frontend tests, 20 protocol/core tests, TypeScript/build pass; 19 deployed socket checks pass. Read-only reviews cover allocation/privacy and collision transforms, grounding and lifecycle. The single existing localhost:4173 preview was reused; no additional game/backend listener was started.
