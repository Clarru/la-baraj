# Female detail correction

The first frozen c60c067 packet remains an unaccepted control. Its close-up exposed
white neutral brows and abrupt added forelock roots; Prone_Idle also exposed hair
overlap/hatching. The passing settings UI review is separate from this asset gate.

The new candidate explicitly tints brows brunette. A material contract rejects the
observed neutral MI_Hair brow factor while accepting eye whites and tinted hair.
Its small negative/positive controls run with:

    node --test scripts/wardrobe-material-contracts.test.mjs

Hair is now fitted from the publisher's measured islands: one outer back drape,
two authored long front locks, and four retained crown/scalp/fringe islands.
The inner scalp stays in its original shape. The added forelock tubes were removed;
their roots no longer have a separate planted cap. The paired authored locks keep
their integrated root and tapered cross sections.

Two additional failures were diagnosed in neutral/rest and idle color-separated
renders: widening the whole bob into a torso envelope created a shoulder flange,
and clamping inner/outer surfaces onto a plane discarded their ordered thickness.
The retained controls are in the ignored wardrobe source working directory.
The corrected drape preserves depth ordering, fits the measured coat surface,
uses its local skin field with a gradual head/torso transition, and adds only the
longitudinal support required by stretched faces. The canonical 65-bone rig,
approved male, clips and renderer/depth settings remain unchanged.

The current export is a **candidate for renewed runtime/adverse-pose inspection**,
not a completed mesh acceptance. Refresh its packed hash/content URL/manifest only
through the normal packer. The frozen c60 runtime/control packet is never replaced.
