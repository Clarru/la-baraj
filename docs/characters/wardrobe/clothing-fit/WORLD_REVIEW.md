## Follow-up on the male tops

Light-colour close-ups exposed dark projected rib artifacts and inadequate rear
waist coverage in lowered poses. The ribs now conform to the reduced shell and
retain their direct fabric material with explicit FabricUV after mesh joining.
The knit uses two 256px rib maps and one additional material draw; the full
body atlas keeps its existing dimensions. Neutral bake gutters avoid black
padding. Lower-waist sections now cover the measured original trouser envelope,
and pelvis weights blend into the torso above the waist. Chest sections remain
within the earlier fitted limits. Both tops required that waist correction.

The render utility now supports a pose focus computed from evaluated visible
skinned vertices. This prevents crouch/prone assets falling outside a fixed
standing camera. Earlier cropped controls are retained locally; they do not
count as fit acceptance. final-male-tops/ contains exact-hash colour and adverse
pose captures, plus the old frozen knit as a matched before control.

New artifact tests reject a rear hem inside the original trouser waistband and
reject rib textures sampling a missing/constant UV channel after export.
The female, support-skin and canvas bytes remain identical to 4db6aa1. Their
unchanged functional evidence can be reused with that qualification. The changed
tops still require their own final additive delta review before release.

No physical iPhone heat/battery measurement has been made for this asset rework.
