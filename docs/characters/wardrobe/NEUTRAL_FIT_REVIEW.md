# Men's neutral fit review

A fresh independent continuation of the Appearance finish review inspected all
18 actual-export front/rear sheets in the second combination pass. The manifest
pins the frozen source GLB hashes, 243 choices and Idle_Neutral frame 1.

Disposition at this scope: **neutral-fit pass**. No material new fit failure was
observed across the nine hair/hat families and 27 clothing/footwear combinations
per family. Tall, ankle and low-shoe variants meet their intended trouser/skin
boundaries; shorts expose the designed shin region; no exposed skin through
opaque boots, detached shoe/leg gap, crown penetration or new torso/hem break was
observed. Existing approved male neck/collar traits were preserved, not recast as
new failures.

Inspected: combinations-00-front/rear through combinations-08-front/rear,
inclusive, under assets/source-models/wardrobe/combination-review-v2/. Its
manifest.json records each PNG hash, source hash and choice index.

This is neutral geometry evidence only. It does not accept action deformation,
actual Game/controller interaction, seats, vehicles, physics or the female mesh.
The named shipped reviewer role was unavailable; this was a fresh independent
fallback reviewer, not a human review.
