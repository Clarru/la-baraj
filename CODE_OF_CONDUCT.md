# Community conduct

Help keep La Baraj welcoming. Be respectful, give constructive feedback, and respect other people's privacy and consent. Harassment, discrimination, threats, and publishing another person's private information are not acceptable.

Maintainers may remove content or limit participation to protect the community. For a private security concern, use the reporting process in [SECURITY.md](SECURITY.md). Conduct concerns can be brought to the repository owner privately through an available GitHub contact channel; do not post someone else's private information in an issue.
